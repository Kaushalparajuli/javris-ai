Jarvis

Jarvis is a voice research assistant for macOS, made by GSoft Technologies. Talk to it naturally, and it researches topics, writes reports, manages your email and calendar, and runs routines for you.

What Jarvis does

  • Voice conversation: speak to Jarvis in real time and get spoken answers with live transcripts.
  • Research in the background: ask for research and Jarvis writes a report with sources while you keep working.
  • Email and calendar help: with your permission, Jarvis can find and summarize emails, draft replies, and add events to your calendar.
  • Routines: set up repeating tasks, like a weekly summary of news in your field.
  • Help with what's on screen: select text in any app and ask Jarvis to explain or rewrite it.

How Jarvis uses your Google data

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you connect one, Jarvis asks for these permissions and uses them only for the features below:

PermissionWhy Jarvis needs it
Read your Gmail (gmail.readonly)To search and summarize emails when you ask, for example "what did Sita send me about the invoice?"
Compose Gmail (gmail.compose)To write drafts and send emails. Jarvis always shows you the email and waits for you to approve it before sending.
Calendar events (calendar.events)To show your schedule and create events or invites you ask for, after you approve them.
Basic profile (email address)To show which Google account is connected.

Jarvis runs on your Mac. GSoft Technologies does not run servers for Jarvis and never receives, stores, or sells your Google data. Google data is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full Jarvis Privacy Policy.

Requirements

macOS 13 or later, a Google Gemini API key, and a ChatGPT account for the research worker.
